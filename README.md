# Tower of Promise
**Strategy and Speed: Run and Innovate with Your Automaton!**

Race to the top of a mysterious tower to claim a legendary treasure! In this spectacular steampunk world, speed alone won't get you there — you'll need to be clever too. Rely on your automaton companion, capable of generating a substance that shifts between solid, liquid, and gas states depending on the situation.

Use this unique ability to overcome obstacles:

- In **solid mode**, the automaton becomes a temporary platform.  
- In **gaseous mode**, it propels the player upward.  
- In **liquid mode**, it slows the player’s fall to prevent death from high drops.  

Combine strategy and quick reflexes in this fast-paced action-platformer!

## Personal Contribution 

I was responsible for the automaton companion's mechanics, including the state-changing behavior (solid, liquid, gaseous) triggered by player input, and the associated physics interactions:

- In **solid mode**, the companion acts as a platform.  
- In **gaseous mode**, it lifts the player upward.  
- In **liquid mode**, it slows the player's fall to prevent fatal impact.

I also developed the **respawn system** for the companion after the player's death. Due to time constraints, we couldn't implement the full animation and arrival sequence for the companion. As a workaround, we designed the logic so that the automaton either follows the player or teleports to them if too far away — while maintaining the intended gameplay mechanics.
